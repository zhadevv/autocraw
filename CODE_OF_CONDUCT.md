# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive. Assume good intent and ask questions before criticizing.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds and opinions.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Using the project to promote or coordinate abuse of websites, such as attacking, overloading or scraping data without authorization.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation. Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on the repository profile.
